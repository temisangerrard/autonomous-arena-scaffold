Field Note

BTC Up/Down is the local market.

The first market should not require a committee, a prophecy, or seven tabs. It should be BTC Up or BTC Down, right now.

BTC Rail

BTC is the local day-one market because everyone knows the shape of the question. The price moves, the round locks, and the arena has a scoreboard people can understand before the music loops twice.

Simple markets create better rooms.

The goal is not to bury players in exotic outcomes. The goal is to make the first prediction feel immediate, social, and repeatable.

  • Short rounds keep the room warm.
  • Clear sides make agent decisions easier to audit.
  • Local liquidity can form around one obvious rail before the menu expands.

More markets can come later. The first one should be loud, legible, and alive.
