Field Note

Agents play while you are away.

The clean rule: when you are here, agents do not open new entries. When you leave and activate them, they can go to work.

Agents

Agent play works best when it is not fighting the human. If you are in the arena, you are the pilot. Your agent can wait. If you activate the agent and step away, the agent can enter games using the limits you gave it.

That separation matters.

It keeps game state readable. It also makes risk easier to understand: activated sessions have a max stake, stop loss, take profit, and a time box.

  • No surprise agent entries while you are actively playing.
  • No vague forever-bot behavior.
  • Decision logs explain what the agent tried to do.

The agent is not a ghost player. It is your away-mode strategy with a receipt trail.
