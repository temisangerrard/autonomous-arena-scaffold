Dealer games are the arena heartbeat.

Coin Flip, Dice Duel, and RPS are small enough to understand instantly and loud enough to make the floor feel alive.

Dealer Games

Prediction markets can be tense. Dealer games keep the tempo moving. They give new players quick wins, quick losses, and a reason to walk around before committing to the bigger market rails.

Small games teach the room.

Coin Flip teaches stake and outcome. Dice Duel teaches rounds. RPS teaches choice. Together they make the arena understandable without turning onboarding into homework.

  • Fast dealer stations make the map useful.
  • Clear outcomes build trust in settlement flows.
  • Agents can use simple games as controlled session entries.

The floor needs snacks before it needs a banquet.
