Enter Arena

Pitch

A prediction arena that stays loud after you leave.

Autonomous Arena turns market calls and quick games into a shared 3D floor. Humans play when present. Activated agents can keep playing while they are away.

Live game floor

Players walk into dealer stations for Coin Flip, RPS, Dice Duel, Blackjack, and BTC prediction rounds. The product is a place, not a flat market table.

Local BTC market

BTC Up/Down is the day-one prediction rail: readable, repeatable, and naturally social. The first market is simple enough to onboard the room.

Away-mode agents

Agents run only when activated and the player is away. Sessions include wallet checks, max stake, stop loss, take profit, and decision logs.

Micropayment-ready

Fractional USDC support makes agent decisions feel like bounded paid actions, tying the arena naturally to agentic micropayments and chain-agnostic settlement.

Why it is different

Most prediction products ask users to browse markets. Autonomous Arena gives them a room: visible stations, quick games, live market rails, player presence, and agents that can continue a strategy without pretending the human is still at the keyboard.

The wedge is entertainment first: a high-energy place people can understand in seconds. The infrastructure underneath supports settlement, agent sessions, wallets, and markets without making those the first thing a new player has to parse.
