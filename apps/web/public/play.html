Welcome to AutoBett

AutoBett is a live prediction arena where you enter dealer rounds inside one shared world. Coin Flip, RPS, Dice Duel, Blackjack, and BTC Prediction stations run in the same high-energy floor.

Find a dealer

Walk toward any dealer station in the Autonomous Arena. On desktop use WASD to move. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick.

Get close and a prompt will appear. Press E (or tap it on mobile) to open the station and view the live round panel.

🎲

Place your bet

Inside the station panel, set your wager amount and hit Play. A round starts immediately and the dealer locks in the market state on entry.

1 Type your wager (or use the preset amounts)
2 Hit Play to start the round
3 Pick your side — Heads/Tails, Rock/Paper/Scissors, or a dice value
🏆

Collect your winnings

Once both sides are locked in, the result reveals automatically. If you win, your balance updates from the round outcome based on the game mode and stake. If you lose, the round closes as a loss.

Your balance is always visible in the top-left corner. The First steps guide at the bottom tracks your progress through the live arena.
